## What is an Exchange?

An exchange is the replacement of an item that the customer ordered with another item.

A merchant creates the exchange, specifying which items to return and which new items to send.

The [OrderExchange data model](/resources/references/order/models/OrderExchange) represents an exchange.

***

## Returned and New Items

When an exchange is created, a return, represented by the [Return data model](/resources/references/order/models/Return), is also created to handle receiving the items back from the customer.

<Note>
  Refer to the [Returns guide](/resources/commerce-modules/order/return) to learn more about returns and how they work.
</Note>

The [OrderExchangeItem data model](/resources/references/order/models/OrderExchangeItem) represents the new items to be sent to the customer. It's associated with the `OrderExchange` data model.

***

## Exchange Shipping Methods

An exchange has shipping methods used to send the new items to the customer. They’re represented by the [OrderShippingMethod data model](/resources/references/order/models/OrderShippingMethod).

The shipping methods for the returned items are associated with the exchange's return, as explained in the [Returns guide](/resources/commerce-modules/order/return#return-shipping-methods).

***

## Exchange Payment

The `OrderExchange` data model has a `difference_due` property that stores the outstanding amount.

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>
        Condition
      </th>

      <th>
        Result
      </th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        `difference_due < 0`
      </td>

      <td>
        The merchant owes the customer a refund of the `difference_due` amount.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        `difference_due > 0`
      </td>

      <td>
        The merchant requires additional payment from the customer of the `difference_due` amount.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        `difference_due = 0`
      </td>

      <td>
        No payment processing is required.
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Any payments or refunds made are stored in the [OrderTransaction data model](/resources/references/order/models/OrderTransaction).

***

## How Exchanges Impact an Order’s Version

When an exchange is confirmed, the order’s [version](/resources/commerce-modules/order/order-versioning) is incremented.
