---
title: UpdateProductTypeDTO
---

The data to update in a product type. The `id` is used to identify which product type to update.

**UpdateProductTypeDTO**

- `value`: `string` (optional) — The new value of the product type.
- `metadata`: [MetadataType](/resources/references/product/types/productMetadataType) (optional) — Holds custom data in key-value pairs.
