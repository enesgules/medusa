## deleteLineItemTaxLines(ids, sharedContext?): Promise\<void>

This method deletes line item tax lines by their IDs.

### Example

```ts
await cartModuleService.deleteLineItemTaxLines([
  "calitxl_123",
  "calitxl_321",
])
```
