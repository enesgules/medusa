---
title: FindOrCreateCustomerStepInput
---

The details of the customer to find or create.

**FindOrCreateCustomerStepInput**

- `customerId`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The ID of the customer to find.
- `email`: `null` | `string` (optional) — If the `customerId` isn't specified, find a customer with this email or create a new customer having this email.
