The details of the promotion codes to apply on a cart.

**GetPromotionCodesToApplyStepInput**

- `cart`: `object` — The cart containing items and shipping methods.
  - `items`: `object`\[] (optional) — The cart's items and the promotion adjustments applied to them.
    - `adjustments`: `object`\[] (optional)
  - `shipping_methods`: `object`\[] (optional) — The cart's shipping methods and the promotion adjustments applied to them.
    - `adjustments`: `object`\[] (optional)
- `promo_codes`: `string`\[] (optional) — the promotion codes to be applied to the cart.
- `action`: [ADD](/resources/references/utils/PromotionUtils/enums/utilsPromotionUtilsPromotionActions#add) | [REMOVE](/resources/references/utils/PromotionUtils/enums/utilsPromotionUtilsPromotionActions#remove) | [REPLACE](/resources/references/utils/PromotionUtils/enums/utilsPromotionUtilsPromotionActions#replace) (optional) — Whether to add, remove, or replace promotion codes.
