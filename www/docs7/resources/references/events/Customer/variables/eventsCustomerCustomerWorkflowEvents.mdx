---
title: CustomerWorkflowEvents
---

- `CustomerWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"customer.created"` (default: "customer.created") — Emitted when a customer is created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"customer.updated"` (default: "customer.updated") — Emitted when a customer is updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"customer.deleted"` (default: "customer.deleted") — Emitted when a customer is deleted.
