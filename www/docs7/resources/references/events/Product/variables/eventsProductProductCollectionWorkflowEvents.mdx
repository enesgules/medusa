---
title: ProductCollectionWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductCollectionWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"product-collection.created"` (default: "product-collection.created") — Emitted when product collections are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"product-collection.updated"` (default: "product-collection.updated") — Emitted when product collections are updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"product-collection.deleted"` (default: "product-collection.deleted") — Emitted when product collections are deleted.
