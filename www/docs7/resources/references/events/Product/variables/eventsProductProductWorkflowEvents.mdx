---
title: ProductWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `UPDATED`: `"product.updated"` (default: "product.updated") — Emitted when products are updated.
  - `CREATED`: `"product.created"` (default: "product.created") — Emitted when products are created.
  - `DELETED`: `"product.deleted"` (default: "product.deleted") — Emitted when products are deleted.
