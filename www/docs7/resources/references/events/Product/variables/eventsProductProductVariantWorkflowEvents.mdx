title: ProductVariantWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductVariantWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `UPDATED`: `"product-variant.updated"` (default: "product-variant.updated") — Emitted when product variants are updated.
  - `CREATED`: `"product-variant.created"` (default: "product-variant.created") — Emitted when product variants are created.
  - `DELETED`: `"product-variant.deleted"` (default: "product-variant.deleted") — Emitted when product variants are deleted.
