---
title: ProductTypeWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductTypeWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `UPDATED`: `"product-type.updated"` (default: "product-type.updated") — Emitted when product types are updated.
  - `CREATED`: `"product-type.created"` (default: "product-type.created") — Emitted when product types are created.
  - `DELETED`: `"product-type.deleted"` (default: "product-type.deleted") — Emitted when product types are deleted.
