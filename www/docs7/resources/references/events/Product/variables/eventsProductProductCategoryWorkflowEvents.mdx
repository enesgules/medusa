---
title: ProductCategoryWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductCategoryWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"product-category.created"` (default: "product-category.created") — Emitted when product categories are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"product-category.updated"` (default: "product-category.updated") — Emitted when product categories are updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"product-category.deleted"` (default: "product-category.deleted") — Emitted when product categories are deleted.
