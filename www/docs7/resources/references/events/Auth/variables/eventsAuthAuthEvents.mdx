---
title: AuthEvents
---

- `AuthEvents`: `object`
  - `MFA_ENABLED`: `"auth.mfa_enabled"` (default: "auth.mfa\_enabled") — Emitted when an MFA factor is enabled for an auth identity.
  - `MFA_DISABLED`: `"auth.mfa_disabled"` (default: "auth.mfa\_disabled") — Emitted when an MFA factor is disabled for an auth identity.
  - `MFA_RECOVERY_CODES_GENERATED`: `"auth.mfa_recovery_codes_generated"` (default: "auth.mfa\_recovery\_codes\_generated") — Emitted when recovery codes are generated for an auth identity.
