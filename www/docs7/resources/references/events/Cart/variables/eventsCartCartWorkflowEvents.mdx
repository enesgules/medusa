---
title: CartWorkflowEvents
---

- `CartWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"cart.created"` (default: "cart.created") — Emitted when a cart is created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"cart.updated"` (default: "cart.updated") — Emitted when a cart's details are updated.
  - `CUSTOMER_UPDATED`: `"cart.customer_updated"` (default: "cart.customer\_updated") — Emitted when the customer in the cart is updated.
  - `REGION_UPDATED`: `"cart.region_updated"` (default: "cart.region\_updated") — Emitted when the cart's region is updated. This event is emitted alongside the `cart.updated` event.
  - `CUSTOMER_TRANSFERRED`: `"cart.customer_transferred"` (default: "cart.customer\_transferred") — Emitted when the customer in the cart is transferred.
