title: InventoryItemWorkflowEvents
---

- `InventoryItemWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"inventory-item.created"` (default: "inventory-item.created") — Emitted when inventory items are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"inventory-item.updated"` (default: "inventory-item.updated") — Emitted when inventory items are updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"inventory-item.deleted"` (default: "inventory-item.deleted") — Emitted when inventory items are deleted.
