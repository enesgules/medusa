---
title: CampaignBudgetUsageContext
---

The context passed when promotion use is registered, reverted or limit is checked.

- `CampaignBudgetUsageContext`: `object` — The context passed when promotion use is registered, reverted or limit is checked.
  - `customer_id`: `string` | `null` — The ID of the customer.
  - `customer_email`: `string` | `null` — The email of the customer.
