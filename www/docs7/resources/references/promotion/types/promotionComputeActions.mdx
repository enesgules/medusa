---
title: ComputeActions
---

A compute action informs you what adjustment must be made to a cart item or shipping method.

- `ComputeActions`: [AddItemAdjustmentAction](/resources/references/promotion/interfaces/promotionAddItemAdjustmentAction) | [RemoveItemAdjustmentAction](/resources/references/promotion/interfaces/promotionRemoveItemAdjustmentAction) | [AddShippingMethodAdjustment](/resources/references/promotion/interfaces/promotionAddShippingMethodAdjustment) | [RemoveShippingMethodAdjustment](/resources/references/promotion/interfaces/promotionRemoveShippingMethodAdjustment) | [CampaignBudgetExceededAction](/resources/references/promotion/interfaces/promotionCampaignBudgetExceededAction) | [PromotionLimitExceededAction](/resources/references/promotion/interfaces/promotionPromotionLimitExceededAction) — A compute action informs you what adjustment must be made to a cart item or shipping method.
  - `action`: `"addItemAdjustment"` — The type of action.
  - `item_id`: `string` — The associated item's ID.
  - `amount`: [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/promotion/types/promotionBigNumberInput) — The amount to remove off the item's total.
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
    - `numeric`: `number`
    - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/promotion/types/promotionBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
  - `is_tax_inclusive`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether the promotion amount includes tax.
  - `code`: `string` — The promotion's code.
  - `description`: `string` (optional) — The promotion's description.
  - `adjustment_id`: `string` — The associated adjustment's ID.
  - `shipping_method_id`: `string` — The associated shipping method's ID.
