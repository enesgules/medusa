---
title: RemoveShippingMethodAdjustment
---

This action indicates that an adjustment must be removed from a shipping method. For example, remove the free shipping discount applied before.

**RemoveShippingMethodAdjustment**

- `action`: `"removeShippingMethodAdjustment"` — The type of action
- `adjustment_id`: `string` — The associated adjustment's ID.
- `shipping_method_id`: `string` — The associated shipping method's ID.
- `code`: `string` — The promotion's code.
