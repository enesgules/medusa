---
title: AddShippingMethodAdjustment
---

This action indicates that an adjustment must be made on a shipping method. For example, make the shipping method free.

**AddShippingMethodAdjustment**

- `action`: `"addShippingMethodAdjustment"` — The type of action.
- `shipping_method_id`: `string` — The associated shipping method's ID.
- `amount`: [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/promotion/types/promotionBigNumberInput) — The amount to remove off the shipping method's total.
  - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `numeric`: `number`
  - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/promotion/types/promotionBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
- `code`: `string` — The promotion's code.
- `description`: `string` (optional) — The promotion's description.
