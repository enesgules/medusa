The capture to be created.

**CreateCaptureDTO**

- `amount`: [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentBigNumberInput) (optional) — The amount of the capture.
  - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `numeric`: `number`
  - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
- `payment_id`: `string` — The associated payment's ID.
- `captured_by`: `string` (optional) — Who captured the payment. For example, a user's ID.
- `is_captured`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether the capture was automatically captured.
