**UpdateAccountHolderDTO**

- `id`: `string` — The ID of the account holder.
- `provider_id`: `string` — The provider's ID.
- `context`: [PaymentProviderContext](/resources/references/payment/interfaces/paymentPaymentProviderContext) & `object` — Necessary context data for the associated payment provider.
  - `account_holder`: [PaymentAccountHolderDTO](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentPaymentAccountHolderDTO) (optional) — The account holder information, if available for the payment provider.
    - `data`: `Record<string, unknown>` — Account holder's details as stored in the third-party payment provider.
  - `customer`: [PaymentCustomerDTO](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentPaymentCustomerDTO) (optional) — The customer information from Medusa.
    - `id`: `string` — The ID of the customer in Medusa.
    - `email`: `string` — The email of the customer.
    - `company_name`: `string` | `null` (optional) — The customer's company name.
    - `first_name`: `string` | `null` (optional) — The customer's first name.
    - `last_name`: `string` | `null` (optional) — The customer's last name.
    - `phone`: `string` | `null` (optional) — The customer's phone number.
    - `billing_address`: [PaymentAddressDTO](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentPaymentAddressDTO) | `null` (optional) — The customer's billing address.
  - `idempotency_key`: `string` (optional) — Idempotency key for the request, if the payment provider supports it. It will be ignored otherwise.
  - `account_holder`: [PaymentAccountHolderDTO](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentPaymentAccountHolderDTO) — The account holder information from Medusa.
    - `data`: `Record<string, unknown>` — Account holder's details as stored in the third-party payment provider.
- `metadata`: `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Holds custom data in key-value pairs.
