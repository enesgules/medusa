The refund to be created.

**CreateRefundDTO**

- `amount`: [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentBigNumberInput) (optional) — The amount of the refund.
  - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `numeric`: `number`
  - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
- `payment_id`: `string` — The associated payment's ID.
- `refund_reason_id`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The associated refund reason's ID.
- `note`: `string` (optional) — A text field that adds some information about the refund
- `created_by`: `string` (optional) — Who refunded the payment. For example, a user's ID.
- `metadata`: `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Holds custom data in key-value pairs.
