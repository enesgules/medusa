---
title: WorkflowResult
---

The details of a workflow's execution.

- `WorkflowResult`: `object` — The details of a workflow's execution.
  - `errors`: [TransactionStepError](/resources/references/workflows/classes/workflowsTransactionStepError)\[] — Any errors that occurred in the workflow.
    - `action`: `string`
    - `handlerType`: [TransactionHandlerType](/resources/references/workflows/enums/workflowsTransactionHandlerType)
    - `error`: `any`
  - `transaction`: [DistributedTransaction](/resources/references/workflows/classes/workflowsDistributedTransaction) — The transaction details of the workflow's execution.
    - `#private`: `any`
    - `flow`: `any`
    - `handler`: [TransactionStepHandler](/resources/references/workflows/types/workflowsTransactionStepHandler)
    - `payload`: `any` (optional)
    - `modelId`: `string`
    - `transactionId`: `string`
    - `runId`: `string`
    - `errors`: `any`
    - `context`: `any`
    - `keyValueStore`: `any`
    - `keyPrefix`: `string`
  - `result`: TResult — The result returned by the workflow.
