---
title: UpdateMoneyAmountDTO
---

The data to update in a money amount. The `id` is used to identify which money amount to update.

**UpdateMoneyAmountDTO**

- `id`: `string` — The ID of the money amount to update.
- `currency_code`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The code of the currency to associate with the money amount.
- `amount`: [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/pricing/types/pricingBigNumberInput) (optional) — The price of this money amount.
  - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `numeric`: `number`
  - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/pricing/types/pricingBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
- `min_quantity`: `null` | [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/pricing/types/pricingBigNumberInput) (optional) — The minimum quantity required to be purchased for this money amount to be applied.
  - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `numeric`: `number`
  - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/pricing/types/pricingBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
- `max_quantity`: `null` | [BigNumberInput](/resources/references/pricing/types/pricingBigNumberInput) (optional) — The maximum quantity required to be purchased for this money amount to be applied.
  - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `numeric`: `number`
  - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/pricing/types/pricingBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `value`: `string` | `number`
  - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
