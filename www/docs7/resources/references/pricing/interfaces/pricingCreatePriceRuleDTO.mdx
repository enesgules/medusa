title: CreatePriceRuleDTO
---

A price rule to create.

**CreatePriceRuleDTO**

- `price_set_id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the associated price set.
- `attribute`: `string` — The attribute of the price rule
- `value`: `string` — The value of the price rule.
- `priority`: `number` (optional) — The priority of the price rule in comparison to other applicable price rules.
- `price_id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the associated price.
