---
title: UpdatePriceRuleDTO
---

The data to update in a price rule. The `id` is used to identify which price rule to update.

**UpdatePriceRuleDTO**

- `id`: `string`
- `price_set_id`: `string` (optional)
- `attribute`: `string` (optional) — The attribute of the price rule
- `value`: `string` (optional) — The value of the price rule.
- `priority`: `number` (optional) — The priority of the price rule in comparison to other applicable price rules.
- `price_id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the associated price.
- `price_list_id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the associated price list.
