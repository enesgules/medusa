title: CalculateShippingOptionsPricesWorkflowOutput
---

The calculated shipping option prices.

- `CalculateShippingOptionsPricesWorkflowOutput`: [CalculatedShippingOptionPrice](/resources/references/types/types/typesCalculatedShippingOptionPrice)\[] — The calculated shipping option prices.
  - `calculated_amount`: `number` — The calculated price.
  - `is_calculated_price_tax_inclusive`: `boolean` — Whether the calculated price includes taxes. If enabled, Medusa will infer the taxes from the calculated price. If false, Medusa will add taxes to the calculated price.
