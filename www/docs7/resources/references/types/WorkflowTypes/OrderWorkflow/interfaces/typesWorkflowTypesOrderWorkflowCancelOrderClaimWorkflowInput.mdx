---
title: CancelOrderClaimWorkflowInput
---

The details of the order claim to be canceled.

## Properties

**CancelOrderClaimWorkflowInput**

- `claim_id`: `string` — The ID of the claim to cancel.
- `no_notification`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether to notify the customer of the cancellation.
- `canceled_by`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the user canceling the claim.
