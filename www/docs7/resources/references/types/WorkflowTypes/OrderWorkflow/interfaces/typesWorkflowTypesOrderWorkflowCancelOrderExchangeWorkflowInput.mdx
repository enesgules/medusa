---
title: CancelOrderExchangeWorkflowInput
---

The details to cancel an order exchange.

## Properties

**CancelOrderExchangeWorkflowInput**

- `exchange_id`: `string` — The ID of the exchange to cancel.
- `no_notification`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether to notify the customer of the cancellation.
- `canceled_by`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the user that's canceling the exchange.
