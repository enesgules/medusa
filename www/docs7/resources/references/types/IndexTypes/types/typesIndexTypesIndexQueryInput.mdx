- `IndexQueryInput`: `object`
  - `entity`: TEntry | keyof [IndexServiceEntryPoints](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/interfaces/typesIndexTypesIndexServiceEntryPoints) — The name of the entity to retrieve. For example, `product`.
  - `fields`: [ObjectToIndexFields](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/types/typesIndexTypesObjectToIndexFields)\<[IndexServiceEntryPoints](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/interfaces/typesIndexTypesIndexServiceEntryPoints)\[TEntry & keyof [IndexServiceEntryPoints](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/interfaces/typesIndexTypesIndexServiceEntryPoints)]> extends `never` ? `string`\[] : [ObjectToIndexFields](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/types/typesIndexTypesObjectToIndexFields)\<[IndexServiceEntryPoints](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/interfaces/typesIndexTypesIndexServiceEntryPoints)\[TEntry & keyof [IndexServiceEntryPoints](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/interfaces/typesIndexTypesIndexServiceEntryPoints)]>\[] | `string`\[] — The fields and relations to retrieve in the entity.
  - `pagination`: `object` (optional) — Pagination configurations for the returned list of items.
    - `skip`: `number` (optional) — The number of items to skip before retrieving the returned items.
    - `take`: `number` (optional) — The maximum number of items to return.
    - `order`: [IndexOrderBy](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/types/typesIndexTypesIndexOrderBy)\<TEntry> (optional) — Sort by field names in ascending or descending order.
  - `filters`: [IndexFilters](/resources/references/types/IndexTypes/types/typesIndexTypesIndexFilters)\<TEntry> (optional) — Filters to apply on the retrieved items.
  - `context`: [QueryContextType](/resources/references/types/CommonTypes/types/typesCommonTypesQueryContextType) (optional) — Apply a query context on the retrieved data. For example, to retrieve product prices for a certain context.
  - `withDeleted`: `boolean` (optional) — Apply a `withDeleted` flag on the retrieved data to retrieve soft deleted items.
