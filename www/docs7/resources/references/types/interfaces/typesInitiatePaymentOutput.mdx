---
title: InitiatePaymentOutput
---

The successful result of initiating a payment session using a third-party payment provider.

## Properties

**InitiatePaymentOutput**

- `data`: `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — The unstructured data returned from the payment provider. The content will vary between providers.
- `id`: `string` — The ID of the payment session in the payment provider.
- `status`: [PaymentSessionStatus](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentPaymentSessionStatus) (optional) — The status of the payment session, which will be stored in the payment session's `status` field.
