## Properties

**IdentifyGroupDTO**

- `group`: `object` — The group that the event is for, such as an organization or team.
  - `type`: `string` — The name of the group.

    Example:

    ```
    "organization"
    ```
  - `id`: `string` — The group's ID.
- `actor_id`: `string` (optional) — When identifying a group, the actor can potentially be passed as well as metadata.
- `properties`: `Record<string, any>` (optional) — The properties of the group. The format and content is dynamic and depends on the integrated provider.
