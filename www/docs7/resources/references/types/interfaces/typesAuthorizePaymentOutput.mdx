---
title: AuthorizePaymentOutput
---

The successful result of authorizing a payment session using a payment provider.

## Properties

**AuthorizePaymentOutput**

- `data`: `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — The unstructured data returned from the payment provider. The content will vary between providers.
- `status`: [PaymentSessionStatus](/resources/references/payment/types/paymentPaymentSessionStatus) — The status of the payment, which will be stored in the payment session's `status` field.
