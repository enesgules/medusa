---
title: RetrieveAccountHolderOutput
---

The result of retrieving an account holder in the third-party payment provider. The `data`
property is stored as-is in Medusa's account holder's `data` property.

## Properties

**RetrieveAccountHolderOutput**

- `data`: `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — The unstructured data returned from the payment provider. The content will vary between providers.
- `id`: `string` — The ID of the account holder in the payment provider.
