---
title: SearchCountStrategy
---

How the total number of matching documents is computed:

- `estimated`: an approximate count, which is what most engines return.

- `exact`: an exact count, which may be rejected or slow depending on the provider.

- `none`: no count at all, making the result's `count` `null`.

- `SearchCountStrategy`: `"estimated"` | `"exact"` | `"none"` — How the total number of matching documents is computed: - `estimated`: an approximate count, which is what most engines return. - `exact`: an exact count, which may be rejected or slow depending on the provider. - `none`: no count at all, making the result's `count` `null`.
