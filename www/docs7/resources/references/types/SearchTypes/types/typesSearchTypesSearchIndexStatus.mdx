---
title: SearchIndexStatus
---

Status of a search index:

- `pending` — created but not filled

- `building` — a seed or reindex is in progress

- `ready` — serving documents

- `error` — the last seed or reindex failed

- `SearchIndexStatus`: `"pending"` | `"building"` | `"ready"` | `"error"` — Status of a search index: - `pending` — created but not filled - `building` — a seed or reindex is in progress - `ready` — serving documents - `error` — the last seed or reindex failed
