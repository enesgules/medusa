---
title: InferSearchPropertyValue
---

The document value a single search property accepts. Every field may be
`null` — engines treat null as absent, and real documents carry nulls for
missing data.

> **Note**
>
> This is available starting from [Medusa v2.19.0](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa/releases/tag/v2.19.0).

- `InferSearchPropertyValue`: P extends [SearchArrayMarker](/resources/references/types/SearchTypes/interfaces/typesSearchTypesSearchArrayMarker) ? P\[`"$dataType"`]\[] : P\[`"$dataType"`] | `null` — The document value a single search property accepts. Every field may be `null` — engines treat null as absent, and real documents carry nulls for missing data.
