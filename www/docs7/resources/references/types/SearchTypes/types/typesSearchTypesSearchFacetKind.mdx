---
title: SearchFacetKind
---

The type of faceting supported on a field:

- `value`: counts per distinct value.

- `range`: counts per numeric or date range.

- `stats`: aggregations, such as the minimum and maximum, over the field.

- `SearchFacetKind`: `"value"` | `"range"` | `"stats"` — The type of faceting supported on a field: - `value`: counts per distinct value. - `range`: counts per numeric or date range. - `stats`: aggregations, such as the minimum and maximum, over the field.
