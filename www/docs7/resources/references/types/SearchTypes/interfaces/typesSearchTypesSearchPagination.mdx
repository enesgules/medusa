---
title: SearchPagination
---

How a search query's results are paginated and sorted.

## Properties

**SearchPagination**

- `skip`: `number` (optional) — The number of documents to skip before the returned hits. Mutually exclusive with `cursor`.
- `take`: `number` (optional) — The maximum number of hits to return.
- `order`: `Record<string, [SearchOrderBy](/references/types/SearchTypes/types/types.SearchTypes.SearchOrderBy)>` (optional) — The fields to sort the hits by, keyed by the field's dotted path. The reserved `_score` key sorts by relevance.
- `cursor`: `string` (optional) — An opaque provider cursor, as returned in a previous result's `metadata.next_cursor`. Mutually exclusive with `skip`.
