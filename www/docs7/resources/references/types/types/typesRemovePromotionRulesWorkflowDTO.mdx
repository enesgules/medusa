The data to remove rules of a promotion.

- `RemovePromotionRulesWorkflowDTO`: `object` — The data to remove rules of a promotion.

  - `data`: `object` — The data to remove the rules.
    - `id`: `string` — The ID of the promotion to remove its rules.
    - `rule_ids`: `string`\[] — The IDs of the rules to remove.
