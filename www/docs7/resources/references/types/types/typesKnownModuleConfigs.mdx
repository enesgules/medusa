---
title: KnownModuleConfigs
---

Generates a union of typed module configs for all known modules in the ModuleOptions registry.
This distributes over all keys in ModuleOptions to create specific config types for each.

- `KnownModuleConfigs`: [ModuleConfigForResolve](/resources/references/types/types/typesModuleConfigForResolve)\<keyof [ModuleOptions](/resources/references/types/CommonTypes/interfaces/typesCommonTypesModuleOptions) & string> — Generates a union of typed module configs for all known modules in the ModuleOptions registry.
