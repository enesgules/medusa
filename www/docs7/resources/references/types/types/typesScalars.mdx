---
title: Scalars
---

All built-in and custom scalars, mapped to their actual values

- `Scalars`: `object` — All built-in and custom scalars, mapped to their actual values
  - `ID`: `object`
    - `input`: `string`
    - `output`: `string`
  - `String`: `object`
    - `input`: `string`
    - `output`: `string`
  - `Boolean`: `object`
    - `input`: `boolean`
    - `output`: `boolean`
  - `Int`: `object`
    - `input`: `number`
    - `output`: `number`
  - `Float`: `object`
    - `input`: `number`
    - `output`: `number`
  - `DateTime`: `object`
    - `input`: `Date` | `string`
    - `output`: `Date` | `string`
  - `JSON`: `object`
    - `input`: `Record<string, unknown>`
    - `output`: `Record<string, unknown>`
