The options to apply when retrieving an item.

## Type parameters

**OptionsQuery**

- `T`: `object` (optional)

## Properties

**OptionsQuery**

- `populate`: `string`\[] (optional) — Relations to populate in the retrieved items.
- `orderBy`: [Order](/resources/references/types/types/typesOrder)\<T> | [Order](/resources/references/types/types/typesOrder)\<T>\[] (optional) — Fields to sort-order items by.
- `limit`: `number` (optional) — Limit the number of items retrieved in the list.
- `offset`: `number` (optional) — The number of items to skip before the retrieved items in the list.
- `fields`: `string`\[] (optional) — The fields to include in each of the items.
- `groupBy`: `string` | `string`\[] (optional) — Group results by a field or set of fields.
- `filters`: `boolean` | `string`\[] | [Dictionary](/resources/references/types/types/typesDictionary)\<boolean | [Dictionary](/resources/references/types/types/typesDictionary)> (optional) — Filters to apply on the retrieved items.
- `strategy`: `string` & `object` | `"select-in"` | `"joined"` | `"balanced"` (optional) — Load strategy (e.g for mikro orm it accept select-in or joined)
