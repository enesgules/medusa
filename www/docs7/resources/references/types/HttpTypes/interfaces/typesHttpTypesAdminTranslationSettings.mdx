## Properties

**AdminTranslationSettings**

- `id`: `string` — The ID of the settings.
- `created_at`: `string` | `Date` — The date and time the settings were created.
- `updated_at`: `string` | `Date` — The date and time the settings were last updated.
- `deleted_at`: `null` | `string` | `Date` — The date and time the settings were deleted.
- `entity_type`: `string` — The entity type.
- `fields`: `string`\[] — The translatable fields.
- `is_active`: `boolean` — Whether the entity translatable status is enabled.
