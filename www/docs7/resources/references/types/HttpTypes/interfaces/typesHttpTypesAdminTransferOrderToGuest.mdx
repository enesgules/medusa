---
title: AdminTransferOrderToGuest
---

The data to transfer an order to a guest customer.

## Properties

**AdminTransferOrderToGuest**

- `email`: `string` — The email of the guest customer to transfer the order to. If no customer exists with this email, a guest customer is created.
- `internal_note`: `string` (optional) — An internal note viewed by admins only.
- `description`: `string` (optional) — A description for the transfer.
