---
title: AdminSearchIndexReindexResponse
---

The result of triggering a search index reindex. The reindex runs in the
background - check the index's `status` to know when it's done.

## Properties

**AdminSearchIndexReindexResponse**

- `job_id`: `string` — Identifier of the triggered reindex job.
- `indexes`: `string`\[] — Names of the indexes being reindexed.
