## Properties

**AdminCreateSalesChannel**

- `name`: `string` — The sales channel's name.
- `description`: `string` (optional) — The sales channel's description.
- `is_disabled`: `boolean` (optional, default: `false`) — Whether the sales channel is disabled.
- `metadata`: `null` | `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Custom key-value pairs that can be added to the sales channel.
