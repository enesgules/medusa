## Properties

**AdminRbacPolicy**

- `id`: `string` — The policy's ID.
- `key`: `string` — The policy's unique key.
- `resource`: `string` — The resource the policy applies to.
- `operation`: `string` — The operation the policy allows.
- `name`: `null` | `string` — The policy's name.
- `description`: `null` | `string` — The policy's description.
- `metadata`: `null` | `Record<string, unknown>` — Custom key-value pairs that can be added to the policy.
- `inherited_from_role_id`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The role ID the policy is inherited from, if any.
- `created_at`: `string` — The date the policy was created.
- `updated_at`: `string` — The date the policy was updated.
- `deleted_at`: `null` | `string` — The date the policy was deleted.
