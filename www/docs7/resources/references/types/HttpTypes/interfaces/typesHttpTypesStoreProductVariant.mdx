  - `is_calculated_price_tax_inclusive`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether the calculated price is tax inclusive.

  - `is_original_price_price_list`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether the original price is associated with a price list. During the calculation process, if the price list of the calculated price is of type override, the original price will be the same as the calculated price.   - `original_amount_with_tax`: `null` | `number` — The amount of the original price with taxes included. If the original price is tax inclusive, this field will be the same as `original_amount`.
