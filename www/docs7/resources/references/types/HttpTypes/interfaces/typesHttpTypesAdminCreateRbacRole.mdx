## Properties

**AdminCreateRbacRole**

- `name`: `string` — The role's name.
- `parent_id`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The ID of the parent role, if any.
- `description`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The role's description.
- `metadata`: `null` | `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Custom key-value pairs that can be added to the role.
- `policy_ids`: `string`\[] (optional) — The IDs of the policies to attach to the role on creation. The actor must hold every policy in the list; omit or leave empty to create a role without any policies.
