title: AdminAddClaimOutboundItems
---

The details to add outbound items to a claim.

## Properties

**AdminAddClaimOutboundItems**

- `items`: `object`\[] — The outbound items to add to the claim.
  - `variant_id`: `string` — The ID of the product variant.
  - `quantity`: `number` — The quantity of the item.
  - `unit_price`: `number` (optional) — The item's unit price.
  - `internal_note`: `string` (optional) — An internal note viewed by admin users only.
  - `metadata`: `null` | `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Key-value pairs of custom data.
