A list of inventory levels to create, update, or delete.

## Properties

**AdminBatchInventoryItemsLocationLevels**

- `create`: [AdminBatchCreateInventoryItemsLocationLevels](/resources/references/types/HttpTypes/interfaces/typesHttpTypesAdminBatchCreateInventoryItemsLocationLevels)\[] (optional) — The inventory levels to create.
  - `location_id`: `string` — The ID of the associated stock location.
  - `inventory_item_id`: `string` — The ID of the associated inventory item.
  - `stocked_quantity`: `number` (optional) — The associated inventory item's stocked quantity in the associated stock location.
  - `incoming_quantity`: `number` (optional) — The associated inventory item's incoming quantity in the associated stock location.
- `update`: [AdminBatchUpdateInventoryItemsLocationLevels](/resources/references/types/HttpTypes/interfaces/typesHttpTypesAdminBatchUpdateInventoryItemsLocationLevels)\[] (optional) — The inventory levels to update.
  - `location_id`: `string` — The ID of the associated stock location.
  - `inventory_item_id`: `string` — The ID of the associated inventory item.
  - `stocked_quantity`: `number` (optional) — The associated inventory item's stocked quantity in the associated stock location.
  - `incoming_quantity`: `number` (optional) — The associated inventory item's incoming quantity in the associated stock location.
  - `id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the inventory level to update.
- `delete`: `string`\[] (optional) — The IDs of the inventory levels to delete.
- `force`: `boolean` (optional) — If enabled, the inventory levels will be deleted even if they have stocked quantity.
