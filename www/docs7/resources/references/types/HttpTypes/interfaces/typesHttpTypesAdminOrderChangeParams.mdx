---
title: AdminOrderChangeParams
---

The filters to apply when retrieving order changes.

## Properties

**AdminOrderChangeParams**

- `fields`: `string` (optional) — The fields and relations to retrieve separated by commas. Learn more in the [API reference](/api/store/select-fields-and-relations).
- `status`: `string` | `string`\[] (optional) — Filter by change status(es).
- `created_at`: `any` (optional) — Apply filters on the change's creation date.
- `id`: `string` | `string`\[] (optional) — Filter by change ID(s).
- `updated_at`: `any` (optional) — Apply filters on the change's update date.
- `deleted_at`: `any` (optional) — Apply filters on the change's deletion date.
- `change_type`: `string` | `string`\[] (optional) — Filter by change type(s).
