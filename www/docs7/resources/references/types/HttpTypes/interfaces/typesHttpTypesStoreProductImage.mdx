The product image's details.

## Properties

**StoreProductImage**

- `id`: `string` — The image's ID.
- `url`: `string` — The image's URL.
- `rank`: `number` — The rank of the product image.
- `created_at`: `string` (optional) — The date the image was created.
- `updated_at`: `string` (optional) — The date the image was updated.
- `deleted_at`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The date the image was deleted.
- `metadata`: `null` | `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Key-value pairs of custom data.
