The details to update a claim inbound item.

## Properties

**AdminUpdateClaimInboundItem**

- `quantity`: `number` (optional) — The item's claimed quantity.
- `reason_id`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The ID of the associated claim reason.
- `description`: `string` (optional) — The claim item's description.
- `internal_note`: `null` | `string` (optional) — An internal note viewed by admin users only.
