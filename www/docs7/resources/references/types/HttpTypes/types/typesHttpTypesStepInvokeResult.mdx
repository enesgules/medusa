---
title: StepInvokeResult
---

- `StepInvokeResult`: `object`
  - `output`: `object` — The output details of the step.
    - `output`: `unknown` — The output of the step. This is the first parameter passed to the returned `StepResponse` function.
    - `compensateInput`: `unknown` — The input of the step's compensation function. This is the second parameter passed to the returned `StepResponse` function.
