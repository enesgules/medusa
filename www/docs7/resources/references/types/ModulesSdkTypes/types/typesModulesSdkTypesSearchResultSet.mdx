What `query.search` returns: the hydrated entities in `data`, and whatever the
engine reported — hits, scores, facets, counts — in `search_result`.

- `SearchResultSet`: `object` — What `query.search` returns: the hydrated entities in `data`, and whatever the engine reported — hits, scores, facets, counts — in `search_result`.
