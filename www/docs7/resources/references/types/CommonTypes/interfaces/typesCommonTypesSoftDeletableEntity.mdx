---
title: SoftDeletableEntity
---

Representing a deletable entity.

## Properties

**SoftDeletableEntity**

- `id`: `string` — The ID of an entity's record.
- `created_at`: `Date` — The date an entity's record was created.
- `updated_at`: `Date` — The date an entity's record was updated.
- `deleted_at`: `null` | `Date` — The date an entity's record was deleted.
