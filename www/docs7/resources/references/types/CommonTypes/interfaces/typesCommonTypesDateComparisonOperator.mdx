---
title: DateComparisonOperator
---

Fields used to apply flexible filters on dates.

## Properties

**DateComparisonOperator**

- `lt`: `Date` (optional) — The filtered date must be less than this value.
- `gt`: `Date` (optional) — The filtered date must be greater than this value.
- `gte`: `Date` (optional) — The filtered date must be greater than or equal to this value.
- `lte`: `Date` (optional) — The filtered date must be less than or equal to this value.
