---
title: NumericalComparisonOperator
---

Fields used to apply flexible filters on numbers.

## Properties

**NumericalComparisonOperator**

- `lt`: `number` (optional) — The filtered number must be less than this value.
- `gt`: `number` (optional) — The filtered number must be greater than this value.
- `gte`: `number` (optional) — The filtered number must be greater than or equal to this value.
- `lte`: `number` (optional) — The filtered number must be less than or equal to this value.
