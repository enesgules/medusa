The data result of a bulk operation.

- `BatchWorkflowOutput`: [BatchMethodResponse](/resources/references/types/CommonTypes/types/typesCommonTypesBatchMethodResponse)\<T> — The data result of a bulk operation.
  - `created`: T\[] — The records that were created in the bulk operation.
  - `updated`: T\[] — The records that were updated in the bulk operation.
  - `deleted`: `string`\[] — The IDs of the records deleted in the bulk operation.
