title: BatchMethodRequest
---

Data to manage in bulk related to a model.

- `BatchMethodRequest`: `object` — Data to manage in bulk related to a model.
  - `create`: TCreate\[] (optional) — Records to create in bulk.
  - `update`: TUpdate\[] (optional) — Records to update in bulk.
  - `delete`: TDelete\[] (optional) — Records to delete in bulk.
