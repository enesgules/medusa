title: ModuleUpdateStoreCreditAccount
---

The data required to update a store credit account.

- `ModuleUpdateStoreCreditAccount`: `object` — The data required to update a store credit account.
  - `id`: `string` — The ID of the store credit account to update.
  - `customer_id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the customer to associate with the account.
  - `currency_code`: `string` (optional) — The three-letter ISO currency code to update.
  - `metadata`: `Record<string, unknown>` (optional) — Custom key-value pairs to update on the account.
