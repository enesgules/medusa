title: ModuleAccountStats
---

Aggregated balance statistics for a store credit account.

- `ModuleAccountStats`: `object` — Aggregated balance statistics for a store credit account.
  - `id`: `string` — The store credit account's ID.
  - `balance`: [BigNumberValue](/resources/references/store_credit/types/store_creditBigNumberValue) — The current balance of the account (credits minus debits).
    - `numeric`: `number`
    - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/store_credit/types/store_creditBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
  - `credits`: [BigNumberValue](/resources/references/store_credit/types/store_creditBigNumberValue) — The total amount credited to the account.
    - `numeric`: `number`
    - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/store_credit/types/store_creditBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
  - `debits`: [BigNumberValue](/resources/references/store_credit/types/store_creditBigNumberValue) — The total amount debited from the account.
    - `numeric`: `number`
    - `raw`: [BigNumberRawValue](/resources/references/store_credit/types/store_creditBigNumberRawValue) (optional)
    - `bigNumber`: `BigNumber` (optional)
