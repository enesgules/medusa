---
title: UserWorkflowEvents
---

- `UserWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"user.created"` (default: "user.created") — Emitted when users are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"user.updated"` (default: "user.updated") — Emitted when users are updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"user.deleted"` (default: "user.deleted") — Emitted when users are deleted.
