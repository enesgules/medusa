---
title: ProductOptionWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductOptionWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `UPDATED`: `"product-option.updated"` (default: "product-option.updated") — Emitted when product options are updated.
  - `CREATED`: `"product-option.created"` (default: "product-option.created") — Emitted when product options are created.
  - `DELETED`: `"product-option.deleted"` (default: "product-option.deleted") — Emitted when product options are deleted.
