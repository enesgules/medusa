---
title: ProductTagWorkflowEvents
---

- `ProductTagWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `UPDATED`: `"product-tag.updated"` (default: "product-tag.updated") — Emitted when product tags are updated.
  - `CREATED`: `"product-tag.created"` (default: "product-tag.created") — Emitted when product tags are created.
  - `DELETED`: `"product-tag.deleted"` (default: "product-tag.deleted") — Emitted when product tags are deleted.
