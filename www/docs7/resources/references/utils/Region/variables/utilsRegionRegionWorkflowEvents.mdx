---
title: RegionWorkflowEvents
---

- `RegionWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `UPDATED`: `"region.updated"` (default: "region.updated") — Emitted when regions are updated.
  - `CREATED`: `"region.created"` (default: "region.created") — Emitted when regions are created.
  - `DELETED`: `"region.deleted"` (default: "region.deleted") — Emitted when regions are deleted.
