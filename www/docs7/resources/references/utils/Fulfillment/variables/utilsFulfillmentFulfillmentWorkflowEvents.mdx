---
title: FulfillmentWorkflowEvents
---

- `FulfillmentWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `SHIPMENT_CREATED`: `"shipment.created"` (default: "shipment.created") — Emitted when a shipment is created for an order.
  - `DELIVERY_CREATED`: `"delivery.created"` (default: "delivery.created") — Emitted when a fulfillment is marked as delivered.
