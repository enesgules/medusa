---
title: SalesChannelWorkflowEvents
---

- `SalesChannelWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"sales-channel.created"` (default: "sales-channel.created") — Emitted when sales channels are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"sales-channel.updated"` (default: "sales-channel.updated") — Emitted when sales channels are updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"sales-channel.deleted"` (default: "sales-channel.deleted") — Emitted when sales channels are deleted.
