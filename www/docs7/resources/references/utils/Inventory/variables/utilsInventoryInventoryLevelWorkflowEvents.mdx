---
title: InventoryLevelWorkflowEvents
---

- `InventoryLevelWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"inventory-level.created"` (default: "inventory-level.created") — Emitted when inventory levels are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"inventory-level.updated"` (default: "inventory-level.updated") — Emitted when inventory levels are updated. This includes adjustments to the stocked or reserved quantity, such as during order fulfillment.
  - `DELETED`: `"inventory-level.deleted"` (default: "inventory-level.deleted") — Emitted when inventory levels are deleted.
