---
title: TranslationWorkflowEvents
---

- `TranslationWorkflowEvents`: `object`
  - `CREATED`: `"translation.created"` (default: "translation.created") — Emitted when translations are created.
  - `UPDATED`: `"translation.updated"` (default: "translation.updated") — Emitted when translations are updated.
  - `DELETED`: `"translation.deleted"` (default: "translation.deleted") — Emitted when translations are deleted.
