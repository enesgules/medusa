title: UpsertSalesChannelDTO
---

The attributes in the sales channel to be created or updated.

**UpsertSalesChannelDTO**

- `id`: `string` (optional) — The ID of the sales channel.
- `name`: `string` (optional) — The name of the sales channel. Required when creating a sales channel.
- `description`: `null` | `string` (optional) — The description of the sales channel.
- `is_disabled`: `boolean` (optional) — Whether the sales channel is disabled.
- `metadata`: [MetadataType](/resources/references/sales_channel/types/sales_channelMetadataType) (optional) — Holds custom data in key-value pairs.
