---
title: Remove Gift Card
---

`DELETE /store/carts/{id}/gift-cards`

Remove a Gift Card from a cart.

## Base URLs

- `http://localhost:9000`

- `https://api.medusajs.com`

## Authentication

This operation does not require authentication.

[Authentication guide](/api/store/authentication)
