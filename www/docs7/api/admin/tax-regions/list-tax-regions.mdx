---
title: List Tax Regions
---

`GET /admin/tax-regions`

Retrieve a list of tax regions. The tax regions can be filtered by fields such as `id`. The tax regions can also be sorted or paginated.

## Base URLs

- `http://localhost:9000`

- `https://api.medusajs.com`

## Authentication

{
  "api_token": {
    "type": "http",
