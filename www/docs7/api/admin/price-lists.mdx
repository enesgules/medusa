---
title: Price Lists
---

A price list is a group of prices applied if the specified conditions and rules are satisfied.

Price lists are useful for sales or special prices for special conditions, such as applying prices for a set of customers.

These API routes allow admin users to manage price lists.

- [Get a Price List](/api/admin/price-lists/get-a-price-list)
- [List Price Lists](/api/admin/price-lists/list-price-lists)
- [List Prices](/api/admin/price-lists/list-prices)
- [Create Price List](/api/admin/price-lists/create-price-list)
- [Manage Prices](/api/admin/price-lists/manage-prices)
- [Remove Products from Price List](/api/admin/price-lists/remove-products-from-price-list)
- [Update a Price List](/api/admin/price-lists/update-a-price-list)
- [Delete a Price List](/api/admin/price-lists/delete-a-price-list)
