---
title: Product Options
---

A product option is a way to define different options for a product, such as "Size" or "Color". Product options can be created globally and used by multiple products, or they can be exclusive to a specific product.
These API routes allow admin users to manage product options.

- [Get a Product Option](/api/admin/product-options/get-a-product-option)
- [Get Product Option Value](/api/admin/product-options/get-product-option-value)
- [List Product Option Values](/api/admin/product-options/list-product-option-values)
- [List Product Options](/api/admin/product-options/list-product-options)
- [Create Product Option](/api/admin/product-options/create-product-option)
- [Update a Product Option](/api/admin/product-options/update-a-product-option)
- [Update Product Option Value](/api/admin/product-options/update-product-option-value)
- [Delete a Product Option](/api/admin/product-options/delete-a-product-option)
