---
title: Get a Gift Card
---

`GET /admin/gift-cards/{id}`

Retrieve a gift card by its ID. You can expand the gift card's relations or select the fields that should be returned.

## Base URLs

- `http://localhost:9000`

- `https://api.medusajs.com`

## Authentication

```json
{
  "api_token": {
    "type": "http",
```
