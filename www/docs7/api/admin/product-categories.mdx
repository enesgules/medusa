---
title: Product Categories
---

Products can be categorized into categories.

Categories are nested and their heirarchy can be managed, giving admin users flexibility in how they categorize their products.

These API routes allow admin users to manage categories and the products in them.

- [Get a Product Category](/api/admin/product-categories/get-a-product-category)
- [List Product Categories](/api/admin/product-categories/list-product-categories)
- [Create Product Category](/api/admin/product-categories/create-product-category)
- [Manage Products](/api/admin/product-categories/manage-products)
- [Update a Product Category](/api/admin/product-categories/update-a-product-category)
- [Delete a Product Category](/api/admin/product-categories/delete-a-product-category)
