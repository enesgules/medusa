---
title: Get a Product Category
---

`GET /admin/product-categories/{id}`

Retrieve a product category by its ID. You can expand the product category's relations or select the fields that should be returned.

## Base URLs

- `http://localhost:9000`

- `https://api.medusajs.com`

## Authentication

```json
{
  "api_token": {
    "type": "http",
    "x-displayName": "API Token",
    "scheme": "basic"
```
