---
title: Customer Groups
---

Customers can be organized into groups. These groups are useful for segregation and marketing purposes.

For example, you can provide different prices for specific customers by creating a price list conditioned to their group.

These API routes allow admin users to manage groups and the customers in them.

- [Get a Customer Group](/api/admin/customer-groups/get-a-customer-group)
- [List Customer Groups](/api/admin/customer-groups/list-customer-groups)
- [Create Customer Group](/api/admin/customer-groups/create-customer-group)
- [Manage Customers](/api/admin/customer-groups/manage-customers)
- [Update a Customer Group](/api/admin/customer-groups/update-a-customer-group)
- [Delete a Customer Group](/api/admin/customer-groups/delete-a-customer-group)
