title: Sales Channels
---

A sales channel indicates a channel where products can be sold in. For example, a webshop or a mobile app.

These API routes allow admins to manage sales channels and the products available in them.

- [Manage Products](/api/admin/sales-channels/manage-products)
- [Update a Sales Channel](/api/admin/sales-channels/update-a-sales-channel)
- [Delete a Sales Channel](/api/admin/sales-channels/delete-a-sales-channel)
