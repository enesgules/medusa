---
title: Associate Variant's Inventory
---

`POST /admin/products/{id}/variants/{variant_id}/inventory-items`

Associate with a product variant an inventory item that manages its inventory details.

## Base URLs

- `http://localhost:9000`

- `https://api.medusajs.com`
